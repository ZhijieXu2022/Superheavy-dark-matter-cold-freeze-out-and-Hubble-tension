# Superheavy dark matter data and manuscript: cold freeze out and Hubble tension

Data and manuscript for **Xu, Z. (2025), *Cold freeze out of superheavy dark matter and Hubble tension*, Eur. Phys. J. C 85, 1451, doi:[10.1140/epjc/s10052-025-15180-z](https://doi.org/10.1140/epjc/s10052-025-15180-z), arXiv:[2202.07240](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.07240)**.

## Major findings

- A superheavy dark matter particle of mass m<sub>X</sub> ≈ 10<sup>12</sup> GeV, with size r<sub>X</sub> = 4ħ²/(Gm<sub>X</sub><sup>3</sup>) ≈ 10<sup>-13</sup> m, is found by three routes: the free-streaming scale, an "X miracle" analogous to the WIMP miracle, and the scaling laws of dark matter haloes.
- Particles of this mass can form the earliest and smallest gravitationally bound structures at t<sub>X</sub> ≈ 10<sup>-6</sup> s after the Big Bang, and the annihilation or decay in these structures leads to a "cold" freeze out.
- The solutions of the Boltzmann equation show a large depletion, with about one particle in 10<sup>8</sup> surviving as today's dark matter, which gives an additional radiation contribution ΔN<sub>eff</sub> ≈ 0.3–0.4 that could help alleviate the Hubble tension.
- For 10<sup>12</sup> GeV particles the dark coupling constant is α<sub>X</sub> ≈ 0.09, and the instanton-induced decay is consistent with current UHECR bounds.
- The early collapse can release binding energy as gravitational waves of about 100 kHz or as ultralight GUT-scale axions of about 10<sup>-9</sup> eV.
- Superheavy sterile neutrinos are a natural particle realization that links dark matter to neutrino mass and baryogenesis.

## Format of the CSV files

Each file starts with comment lines beginning with `#`, then a header row, then data.

- `# Publisher`: author and contact.
- `# Updated`: date of the last update.
- `#Name`: what the file contains.
- `#Ref.`: the figure or table of the paper the data belong to, with the DOI.
- `#Note`: processing notes, where needed.

Figure files are named by the figure number of the article (for example `Fig07_ke_vs_t_for_illustris_WIMP_X.csv`). Where a figure has more than one panel or series, the columns are named in the header row.
